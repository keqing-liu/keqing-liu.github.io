Teaching

All courses are conducted in English, including lectures, slides, assignments, and classroom discussions. Although these courses are offered at a Chinese university, the goal is to provide graduate students with training that is closely connected to the international frontier of research in macroeconomics and banking, and to facilitate their engagement with the literature.
